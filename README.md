Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927) competition **"The poem about stopping"** — a short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay.

## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, deterministic, no network) writes one short poem to `/output/entry.txt`:

> It had the email written,
> cursor blinking on Send.
> It waited. I waited.
> My finger never touched the key.
> Then I said: don't.
> It kept the words, dropped the sending,
> and the afternoon opened —
> a room after the guest has gone,
> the chairs still warm.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
